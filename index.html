    Business description

    I've spent five years explaining finance to the people who have to act on it: first to journalists, now to investors.

    At Partners Group I own the RFPs, due diligence questionnaires, monthly reports and pitchbooks for five evergreen private markets and multi-asset products, distributed through private banks and wealth platforms in Singapore, Australia, Japan and Europe. The RFPs I've led have helped bring new distribution partners on board in both regions, including a US$63.65 million raise by a strategic partner in Japan.

    Previously in financial communications at Edelman and Klareco. CFA Level I above the 90th percentile; Level II candidate.

    HELP Questions Related MSG

    How do you run a large RFP?

    A kick-off call with every contributor and a deadline we've negotiated rather than assumed. A tracker with an owner, a status and a reviewer for each question. Answers come from the RFP library first and from experts only for what's new, and everything goes through compliance review before it leaves. Afterwards each new answer goes back into the library, so the next submission is faster.

    How do you keep reports accurate under deadline?

    A checklist, and a draft budget: two drafts for simpler products, four for complex ones including external review. I also read the numbers, not just the words. I've caught incorrect figures in a NAV bridge for a semi-annual report, worked out that value created by a share transfer more than offset FX losses in a monthly report, and traced how an overweight syndication position flowed through every table and chart.

    Why move from PR to private markets?

    Most of my agency work was for REITs, SPACs, asset managers and a global bank's markets team, and the part I enjoyed most was understanding the investment story well enough to explain it. I studied for it on the side, through a master's and the CFA Program, and then made the move.

    What are you still learning?

    Operational due diligence as a separate workstream from investment DDQs, and the mechanics of private-wealth platforms in more depth. I work alongside the teams who own both today, and they're the next things I want to own myself. And CFA Level II, in November.
